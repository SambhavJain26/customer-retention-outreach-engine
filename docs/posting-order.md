## Rules this order follows

1. **Never the same build twice in a row.** One audience, four subjects. Rotating is what makes the feed read as a person who works across a property management operation rather than someone with one trick.
2. **Never two carousels, two videos or two images back to back.** Build 2's plan set this rule for itself and it generalizes.
3. **Builds 1 and 2 keep their own numbering.** Both plans state that publishing order is the numbering. The single exception is build 1's ask, moved forward, explained below.
4. **Builds 3 and 4 are resequenced freely.** Both plans say the numbering is for reference and not for publishing order.
5. **Strongest first.** Build 4 post 6, the fair-housing reversal, is described in its own plan as probably the strongest post across all four builds. It opens the run.
6. **The ask recurs roughly every eleven posts**, at slots 12, 23, 34 and 45, rather than four times in the closing stretch.
7. **Runs of three or four text posts are allowed.** Twenty-three of the forty-five remaining posts are text. Breaking every run would mean spending carousels and videos to solve a problem that is not one.

## Decisions this order makes

**Build 2 post 4 is deleted.** "I don't sell software" is build 1 post 5 and build 2 post 4, and it can only be published once. Build 2's plan settles it: if build 1 leads, delete post 4. Build 1 led. Slot 2 publishes the build 1 version and the build 2 copy is retired.

**Build 1's ask moves from last to slot 12.** Four asks across forty-five posts have to be spaced, and build 1's ask is the only one that could move without disturbing a plan that fixes its own order for a reason. That reason was the five-post gateway arc, which is already published. Build 1 posts 13 and 14 trailing its ask costs nothing, because the ask is an offer rather than a conclusion.

**The four asks need rewriting before they go up.** They share a structure by design, and build 2's plan already says the second one to publish must be rewritten rather than refilled or the set reads as templated. Slots 23, 34 and 45 each need a different opening from the one before it. The offer underneath stays the same; only the artifact changes, which is ten certificates, a week of maintenance messages, one slow turn, and a quarter of expirations.

**Nothing gets reshot.** All five remaining video posts are cuts from demo recordings that already exist. Slots 15, 19, 24, 26 and 35 are edits.

## Cadence

At **two a week**, forty-five posts is about twenty-three weeks, a little over five months. At one a week it is ten and a half months, which is too slow for material that is already written.

Two a week is the recommendation. All four plans start at one a week and say to rise to two or three once the audience shifts. It has shifted, and the whole set is drafted.

**If you fall behind, drop text posts, never a carousel or a video.** The order tolerates gaps: skipping a text post shifts everything up by one and breaks none of the rules above, because the format-adjacency rule only constrains the heavy formats.
