Pre-renewal retention / End-to-end workflow

From lease calendar to verified resolution

Trace what the automation coordinates, what AI drafts and triages, and where people decide.

1

Detect & watchlist (120d)

Workflow

Sync PMS lease calendar. Flag upcoming expirations on internal watchlist; check open work orders. No resident message.

2

Prepare & guard (90d)

AI + Rules

Recheck eligibility; AI drafts soft check-in. Fixed rules strictly reject rent, terms, deadlines, money, and legal notices.

3

Obtain manager approval

Manager

Review card in Teams, Slack, or task queue; approve, edit, or skip. No approval means zero messages send.

4

Authenticated dispatch

Workflow

Revalidate fresh PMS state; claim single-send lock; deliver via company mailbox with traceable conversation token.

5

Match reply & triage

AI + Rules

Match reply by token/sender. Sensitive filters catch accommodations and legal flags. AI classifies intent; concerns route to human.

6

Coordinate resolution

Team + Workflow

Link work order or task; manager approves drafted acknowledgement; workflow tracks progress and escalates if overdue.

7

Verify & hand off (30d)

Manager + Leasing

Manager approves confirmation email; resident verifies fix. At 30 days, clean handoff to leasing team with full history.

Who handles what

AI

Drafts conversational check-ins, reads unstructured replies, and extracts grievance categories. Has zero authority to send or commit.

Workflow & rules

Calculates lease windows, enforces wording guardrails, matches replies, reserves single sends, and tracks task SLAs.

Manager & staff

Approves every outbound message, conducts repairs, approves vendor spend, and sets renewal terms in the PMS.

At any stage

Ambiguous identity, accommodation requests, or system failures route immediately to named staff.

Pre-renewal retention / Operations & outcomes

How your team runs it each day

Staff work in familiar tools. Every exception has an owner, and no resident message sends automatically.

Daily operating view

Priority queue. Approvals waiting review, surfaced resident concerns, overdue tasks, and 30-day handoffs.

Quiet logging. Clean positive replies log green. Routine auto-replies log without creating noise.

Daily summary. Digest shows open volume, response speed, waiting work, and stalled items.

Human authority

Staff approve every resident message, dispatch repairs, approve spend, and set renewal terms in the PMS.

Sensitive cases

Accommodation, habitability, discrimination, and legal issues route directly to designated staff.

Controls and failure recovery

Fail-visible architecture

If AI or integrations stall, raw messages route to managers with clear status banners. Send-time guardrails re-verify text even after human edits. Atomic send locks prevent double-sends.

Protect and maintain

Client-dedicated hosting, least-privilege credentials, encrypted storage in transit and at rest, permanent opt-out, watchdog heartbeats, and tested backups.

What this changes for the business

Eliminates ~55 hours/month of manual coordination per 400 expirations. Uncovers resident friction 60 days before the formal renewal letter. Protects resident trust through human-controlled communication.
